About Us

STARLIGHT WOMEN is a group of like-minded women from West African residents in Berlin/Brandenburg Germany. We join forces to create help and support for the LESS PRIVILEGED in our countries of origin, and also here in Berlin/Brandenburg Germany. Starlight Women was formed in November 2021, in Berlin Germany. We are a non-profit organization, with practical social projects aimed at alleviating and improving the social and economic standards of disadvantaged people in our rural communities. These we do through donations in kind, empowerment, workshops on domestic and sexual violence, and support to bereaved women and girls. We also run scholarship projects, especially for people who can not afford basic education. Our goal is to unite us and bring encouragement, and motivation, and to convey inspiration. We are also involved in our immediate community, Berlin/Brandenburg. Here we raise awareness of domestic and sexual violence against women and young girls, and ways of combating it. We also support each other in our community, organizing educational projects in order to create wholeness and well-being. Our goal is to empower people to achieve their life goals.

Mission

Starlight Women is dedicated to empowering marginalized women and girls across Africa through education, advocacy, and inclusive representation. We strive to amplify their voices, foster equality, and provide the necessary resources and support to ensure they achieve their full potential.

Vision

Starlight Women envisions a world where every woman, and girl, regardless of her background has the opportunity to thrive be heard and contribute meaningfully to our society. We aim to create a future where gender equality is a reality and every girl has access to education and empowerment.

Core Values

  • Empowerment
  • Inclusion
  • Education
  • Advocacy
  • Integrity
  • Collaboration
