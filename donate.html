Instructions for making a transfer

How To Donate Via Bank Transfer

  1. Log in to your online banking or visit your bank branch.
  2. Enter the bank details provided above.
  3. Specify the amount you wish to donate.
  4. Use your name or email as the reference so we can confirm your donation.
  5. Complete the transfer.

Let Us Know

After donating, please email us at starlightwomen21@gmail.com with the transfer details so we can confirm receipt and send a thank you.

Contact us if you would like a donation receipt for tax purposes.

Thank you for your generous support! Your contribution helps us continue our work to uplift women and girls in need.

Security and Transparency

We ensure all donations are handled securely, and your contribution will directly support our programs.

Your bank details will be encrypted to avoid any breaching of private information.
